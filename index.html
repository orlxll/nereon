THE SIGNAL

When a workflow keeps stealing attention, it is usually a systems problem.

Too much copyingPeople move the same information between emails, documents, spreadsheets and tools.
Too much waitingRequests sit in inboxes because the next action depends on a person noticing them.
Too much context switchingTeams lose time assembling information before they can make a decision.
Too much manual follow-upThe process works, but only because somebody remembers to keep pushing it forward.
